Free Class Plan: Teach what you know

You do not need a studio or a big following to start. Pick one subject, show one idea in a short reel, run three free demo classes, then charge a fair price that you collect by UPI before every class.

1. Pick one subject

Choose one subject you already know well enough to explain to a beginner. Common options are maths, English, drawing, coding and cooking.

How to choose

  • Start with what people already ask you about. If friends ask you for help with algebra, that is a signal.
  • Pick a level, not everything. "Class 6 maths" works. "Maths" alone is too wide.
  • Pick a result someone can see in a month. For example, "solve linear equations" or "draw a face from the front".
  • Check that you can teach it for 30 minutes without notes.

Write your choice as one sentence: "I teach [level] [subject] to [who], so they can [result]."

2. Record a 1-minute reel

Film one simple idea from your subject. Keep it under 60 seconds, shoot vertical (9:16), and face a window so your face is lit.

Script template (4 lines)

Reel script

Line 1, hook: "Most people get this wrong: [common mistake]."

Line 2, the idea: "The simple way to see it is: [one rule or step]."

Line 3, show it: "Watch me use it on one example: [your example]."

Line 4, invite: "Want a free class like this? Comment CLASS and I will message you."

3. Run three free demo classes

Invite three friends or neighbours. Each demo is 30 minutes, covers one idea, and ends with one small task they can try that day.

  1. Message three people today. Offer one free 30-minute class and ask for a time this week.
  2. Teach one idea. Show the example, then let them try the task.
  3. Send the review request below within an hour of each class.

Review request to copy

Review request

Hi [name], thank you for joining my free class on [subject] today. Could you give me an honest review? Just tell me one thing that helped and one thing I could do better. A short reply is perfect, and the honest parts help me most. Thank you!

4. Set a fair price and take UPI first

Set one price per hour. A fair starting point is what you would expect to pay a tutor for the same help in your area. Check two or three local listings before you decide.

Example only: ₹300 per hour × 10 hours a week = ₹3,000 a week. Your numbers will be different. This is not an income promise.

  • Collect UPI payment before each class starts. No payment, no class.
  • Check the money has arrived in your UPI app before you join the call.
  • State the price in your first message, so nobody is surprised later.

5. Use the first month to build trust

For the first four weeks, the goal is reviews and repeat students, not big money. Keep notes on where people get stuck, reply to every comment, and keep every start time you promise.

By the end of the month you will have reviews to show and notes on what to change.
