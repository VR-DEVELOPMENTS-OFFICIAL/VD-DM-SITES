Free Resume Starter Template

If you write and arrange things neatly, you could help friends, family and neighbours make a clear resume. This guide gives you a clean layout, a template you can copy, three sample types, a price calculator and a checklist for handing over the work. It is the free guide for the keyword RESUME. It is free, and it is not a promise of income.

Before you start

Privacy comes first. A resume holds a person's phone number, email, address and history. Keep every client's personal details private. Never share them with anyone, never post a client's resume or details online, and delete files you no longer need once the work is done.

Start small and start with people you know. A good resume can help someone present themselves well, but it cannot promise a job or an interview, so never tell a client it will. Nobody can tell you in advance how many clients you will get.

Step 1. Choose a clean layout

Employers read a resume quickly. A clean, simple page is easier to read than a crowded one. Keep to one page for a fresher or a student, and at most two pages for someone with several years of work.

  • Name: large and clear at the top.
  • Contact: phone number, email and city. Use a simple, professional email address.
  • Skills: a short list of what the person can really do, with the most useful ones first.
  • Education: course, school or college, and year. Put the latest first.
  • Experience: jobs, internships or projects, with dates and two or three short lines on what the person did. Put the latest first.

Use one simple font, the same size for the same kind of text, and plenty of white space. Skip bright colours and decorations. Check that every date and heading is in the same style. Add a photo only if the employer asks for one.

Step 3. Three sample types

Most clients fall into one of three types. Each type puts a different part first. The names and details below are made up.

1. Fresher

No job yet. Put education, skills, internships and projects first, and write a short summary line.

Fresher sample (made-up)

RAVI KUMAR | Phone: 98000 00001 | Email: ravi.sample@example.com

SUMMARY: Recent B.Sc graduate with good computer and typing skills, looking for an entry-level office role.

EDUCATION: B.Sc (Computer Science), Sample College, 2025. 72%.

SKILLS: MS Word, MS Excel (basic), typing, teamwork.

PROJECT: Built a simple student marks sheet in Excel for a college project.

2. Student

Still studying. Put education first, then skills, and show school or college activities. Mention the kind of part-time work or internship the student wants.

Student sample (made-up)

SNEHA REDDY | Phone: 98000 00002 | Email: sneha.sample@example.com

OBJECTIVE: Second-year B.A. student looking for a weekend part-time job or a summer internship.

EDUCATION: B.A. (second year), Sample College, expected 2027.

SKILLS: Good handwriting, MS Word, social media posting, friendly with people.

ACTIVITIES: Volunteer, college cultural event team, 2025.

3. Job changer

Already working and moving to a different kind of job. Put a summary first, then the skills that carry over, then experience in the order of the latest first.

Job changer sample (made-up)

IMRAN KHAN | Phone: 98000 00003 | Email: imran.sample@example.com

SUMMARY: Shop sales executive with 4 years of experience, now looking for a customer support role.

SKILLS (carried over): talking to customers, solving complaints, billing, calm under pressure.

EXPERIENCE: Sales Executive, Sample Store, 2021 to 2025. Served about 50 customers a day and handled returns and complaints.

EDUCATION: Diploma, Sample Polytechnic, 2020.

Only write what is true. Never add jobs, marks or skills that the client does not have.

Step 4. Work out your price

Decide how much to charge for one resume, based on the time it takes you and what other people near you charge. Count the free tools you use as zero, and add any printing or other cost in the calculator. Compare with other resume services before you decide.

You keep per resume = price - cost per resume
Monthly estimate = you keep per resume × resumes a month

Example only, not a promise: ₹200 a resume × 10 resumes = ₹2,000 a month. Your prices and number of clients will be different, and some months may be slow. Your time is not counted. This is not an income promise.

Step 6. Find your first clients

  • Friends and family: offer to make a resume free for a few people to build samples. Ask each one first before you show their work to anyone.
  • College and job-seeker groups: ask a friend in a college or a local group to share your offer.
  • WhatsApp status: post your price, what is included and how to contact you.

Show only your made-up samples when you promote your service, never a real client's resume.

Copy-and-paste messages

1. Ask for the details

Details request

Hi [name], thank you for choosing me for your resume. Please send: your full name, phone number, email, city, education with years and marks, any jobs or internships with dates, and your skills.

I will keep all your details private and use them only for your resume. I will send a draft by [day].

2. Send the finished resume

Delivery message

Hi [name], your resume is ready as a PDF. Please check every detail carefully: your name, phone number, email, dates and spellings. Tell me if anything needs a change.

The price is ₹[price]. You can pay by UPI. Thank you!

3. Ask for a short review

Review request

Hi [name], I hope the resume works well for you. If you are happy with it, could you write one or two lines as a review? I will not share your resume or personal details. Thank you!

Privacy reminder. Keep clients' personal details private and never share them. Do not post a client's resume or send it to anyone else.
