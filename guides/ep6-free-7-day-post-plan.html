Free 7-Day Post Plan for Local Businesses

Many small shops near you want to post on Instagram or WhatsApp but have no time to plan what to say. You can help them: plan the posts, make them, and post them on time for a fair monthly fee. This guide gives you a five-step method, ready post plans and messages to copy. It is free, and it is not a promise of income.

Before you start

Get the owner's OK before you post any photo that shows a customer, a student or a child. If the owner has not asked them, do not post it. Use photos of the shop, the products, the food and the work instead.

Never buy fake followers or fake likes. They bring no real customers, they can get the account blocked, and an owner who finds out will stop trusting you. Do not promise a number of new customers or followers either. Nobody can know that in advance.

You do not need to invest any money. A phone, free apps and a few hours each week are enough to start. Do not pay for ads with your own money for a shop.

Step 1. Pick one kind of local business

Start with one kind of shop, so you learn what works and can repeat it. Three good first choices are:

  • Salon: haircuts, facials, bridal work. The results look good in photos and short videos.
  • Tuition centre: classes, study tips, results and notices. Parents read these posts.
  • Small restaurant: daily dishes, offers and festival menus. Food is easy to show.

Pick a kind you can visit easily, ideally within walking distance. Look at three shops of that kind. Do they post rarely, or at random times? Those are the ones you can help.

Step 2. Make a simple 7-day post plan

A post plan tells the owner what goes up each day, so nobody has to think about it at the last minute. Mix the types: a photo of the work, a tip, an offer, a short video, and a "meet the team" post. Keep each caption to two or three short lines.

Below are ready plans for three kinds of shop. Change the ideas to fit the real shop you are talking to.

A 7-day plan has seven post ideas, but many owners only want about three posts a week, which is 12 posts a month. Pick the best three each week and use the rest in later weeks.

Step 3. Show the owner, with two example posts

  1. Visit the shop at a quiet time, not at the busiest hour, or send a short message first.
  2. Make two example posts for that shop. Use only what is public, such as the shop board, the menu or your own photo of the shop front, and ask before you take pictures inside.
  3. Show them on your phone and say plainly: "These are free examples. You do not have to buy anything."
  4. Ask what the owner would like to post about, and which days are slow.
  5. Do not pressure anyone. If the answer is no or "later", say thank you and ask again in a month.

The copyable messages further down will help you with the first message.

Step 4. Set a fair monthly price

Decide what you will do and how much it costs, and write it down before the first post. For example: 12 posts a month (the photo or video, a short caption and posting on time), a short weekly report, and a fixed fee. Say what is not included, such as paid ads or a photoshoot.

Example only: ₹2,000 a month for 12 posts for one shop. With 3 shops that would be 3 × ₹2,000 = ₹6,000 a month. Many owners may say no, some will want a lower price, and some will stop after one month. This is not an income promise.

Step 5. Post on time, send a short weekly report, ask for a review

  1. Post at the agreed days and times. Being on time matters more than being perfect.
  2. Each week send the owner a short report: which posts went up, which got the most likes or messages, and what you will try next week.
  3. Keep the report honest. If a week was quiet, say so and say what you will change.
  4. After a few weeks, if the owner is happy, ask for a short, honest review. It helps you show your work to the next shop.
  5. Keep a small notebook: shop, agreed price, what you promised, and the date the owner pays.

1. First message to an owner

First message

Hello [owner's name], I am [your name] from [area]. I saw your [salon / tuition centre / restaurant] and like your [something real, for example "haircut photos" or "dosa"].

I am starting to help local shops plan their Instagram posts. May I make two free example posts for your shop and show them to you? There is no obligation to buy anything.

When would be a good time to show you? Thank you for your time.

2. Message with 2 example posts

Message with examples

Hello [owner's name], here are the two example posts for [shop name] that I promised. They are free, and you can use them if you like.

If you like this style, I can plan 12 posts a month (about 3 a week), post them on the days we agree, and send you a short report every week. My fee would be ₹[price] a month. We can start with one month and see if it suits you.

I will not post any customer photos without your OK. Would you like to talk about it on [day] at [time]?

3. Review request

Review request

Hello [owner's name], thank you for trusting me with your posts this month. I hope they were useful.

If you are happy with the work, could you write one or two honest lines about it that I can show to other shops? If something could be better, please tell me and I will fix it. Thank you!
