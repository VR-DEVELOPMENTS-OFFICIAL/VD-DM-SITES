Before you sell a template, check Canva's licence terms for any photo, element or font you added. Remove anything you are not allowed to sell.

Preview it as a 3-picture reel

People buy what they can see. Post three pictures together as one reel:

  1. Picture 1: the full template on one screen, with the title and price on top.
  2. Picture 2: a close-up of the most useful part, such as the budget table or the checklist.
  3. Picture 3: a short "What is inside" list, with a line telling people how to order.
Caption template

[Template name], ₹99. Comment ORDER or send me a message to get the file.

Pricing example

Keep the price easy to say yes to. A simple starting point is ₹99 per template.

Deliver the file after UPI payment

  1. Ask for payment before you send anything. Share your UPI ID and the exact amount.
  2. Check the payment in your UPI app and wait until the money shows as received.
  3. Send the PDF (and the editable file, if you sell that) in the same chat.
  4. Send a short note: "Payment received. Here is your file. Save it somewhere safe so you can find it again."

Ask for a review

Wait a day after delivery, then send this message:

Review message

Hi [name], thanks for buying the [template name]. Did it work for you? If you have 30 seconds, reply with one honest line about what you liked or what could be better. It helps me a lot. Thank you!
